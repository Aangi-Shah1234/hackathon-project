About us

We're the team
behind the code

A tight-knit crew of engineers, designers, and cloud architects who've shipped products used by millions.

Building the future, one sprint at a time

NexCore was founded in 2019 with one simple idea: great software shouldn't require a 200-person agency. We're a remote-first team of specialists who move fast, communicate clearly, and care deeply about the products we ship.

We've worked with early-stage startups raising their seed round all the way to Fortune 500 engineering teams who needed an expert hand. Every engagement gets our full attention — not a junior subcontractor.

Our edge is the intersection of design thinking and engineering depth. We don't just build what you ask for; we ask the right questions first.
